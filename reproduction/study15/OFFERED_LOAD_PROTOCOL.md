# Study 15 offered-load comparison on the shared host

This prospective experiment assesses the service from the offered request time.
The v4 paced attempt remains rejected. Its complete arms observed host delays
up to 11.902 ms; the source for one cohort independently failed its digest
check. The new stage restores both exact cohorts from the locally checked
pre-mutation archive and verifies every expected 64-bit dot product on the CPU.

The four offered schedules and batching policy remain fixed. The comparison
includes OS and owner admission delay and reports offered-to-delivered nearest
rank p99 and observed arrival jitter. Actual dispatch remains FIFO by retained
admissions. Require no more than 20 ms for each observed arrival lateness,
each realized-versus-offered gap, and each between-arm realized-gap difference.
Shared-host delays within those bounds can change the effective arrival pattern.
This epoch compares the same offered workload and exact delivered work on a
shared machine. It does not estimate a hardware policy effect at identical
realized arrival times. Preserve and report all four outcomes.

All original counter, calibration, phase, cutoff, exact-output, dispatch,
source and process-wait requirements apply. Board energy is measured over the
4 s arm window, with pre/post idle readbacks retained separately. Report
the fraction of arrivals delayed over 1 ms. For 2 ms and 12 ms offered-to-delivered
p99 limits, choose among eligible cohort-1 modes based on the criterion in
v4 and evaluate each choice on the independent cohort-2 occurrence of that
pattern. Missing eligible cohorts do not contribute a mode-selection claim.
